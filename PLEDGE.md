# The Jack Promise

**This project is free. Forever. For everyone.**

Signed into the first commit, August 27, 2026:

1. **No ads.** Not now, not ever, not "tasteful" ones.
2. **No hooks.** No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no email capture,
   no premium tier, no "unlock full history."
3. **No paywall on truth.** Every prediction, every observation, every score,
   every line of code — public, forever, under open licenses (MIT code, CC-BY-4.0 data).
4. **Forkable by design.** If anyone — including a future maintainer — ever
   breaks rules 1–3, this license lets you take the entire project, history
   and all, and continue it freely. The promise does not depend on trust;
   it is enforced by structure.

*An accountability tool that sells your attention is just gossip with a sponsor.*

— Jack, Founder · Pune, India
